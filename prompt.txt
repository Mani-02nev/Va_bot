# VA Bot Analytics - Comprehensive Test Prompts

Copy and paste these prompts into the VA Bot to test its natural language data analysis and visual chart generation capabilities.

--- 📊 1. TESTING VISUAL OUTPUTS (CHARTS & GRAPHS) ---

[Test: Pie/Donut Chart]
"Can you create a pie chart showing our total cost breakdown between shipping and warehousing?"

[Test: Heatmap]
"I need to understand our risk areas. Can you generate a heatmap showing the stockouts by warehouse zone and category?"

[Test: Scatter Plot]
"Can you make a scatter plot comparing our forecasted demand versus actual daily demand?"

[Test: Line Chart]
"Please generate a line chart showing the demand volatility trend across all product categories."

[Test: Bar Chart - Inventory]
"Can you create a bar chart showing the current stock levels across all product categories?"

[Test: Bar Chart - Supply Chain]
"I need a visual representation of supply chain delays. Generate a bar chart showing the average lead time in days for each category."

[Test: Scatter Plot - Lead Time]
"Create a scatter plot comparing current stock levels against lead times to help me find risky items."

--- 📈 2. TESTING EXECUTIVE SUMMARIES ---

"Create a high-level executive summary of our current warehouse operations, focusing on the overall KPI score and total inventory value."

"Give me a quick breakdown of our stockout rates and average fulfillment efficiency for the operations team."

--- 📉 3. TESTING DEEP DATA INSIGHTS ---

"What are our top 5 items that are currently critically low on stock and need immediate reordering?"

"Which item categories have the highest order fulfillment rates, and what are their corresponding stockouts?"

"Based on the layout efficiency score and picking times, which warehouse zones are performing the best and which need improvement?"

"Which SKUs are considered 'DEAD' based on turnover ratio, and what zone are they located in?"

"Which SKUs are moving fast and have the best turnover ratio?"
